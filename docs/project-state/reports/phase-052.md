## 2. Theoretical & Algorithmic Formulation

Phase 52 completes the core routing engine of SABRE (*SWAP-based Bounded-depth Routing Engine*, ASPLOS 2019, Li et al.) by implementing reverse circuit traversal, mathematical operator inversion, and multi-round iterative layout refinement:

### 2.1 Reverse Circuit Traversal & Mathematical Adjoints
In forward routing, initial placement choices made without future sight can lead to costly SWAP overheads early in the circuit. SABRE resolves this through reverse traversal:
1. **Mathematical Adjoint Inversion**: A reversed circuit $C_{\text{rev}} = C^\dagger$ is generated by reversing the topological operation sequence, inverting DAG dependencies, and mapping every unitary quantum operation to its exact mathematical inverse:
   - **Self-Inverse Gates**: $H, X, Y, Z, CX, CZ, SWAP, Id$ retain their canonical operator identity ($U^\dagger = U$).
   - **Discrete Inverse Pairs**: Paired gates map to their catalogued adjoint ($S \leftrightarrow S^\dagger, T \leftrightarrow T^\dagger$).
   - **Continuous Parameterized Rotations**: Continuous rotation gates negate their parameter expressions ($Rx(\theta) \to Rx(-\theta), Ry(\theta) \to Ry(-\theta), Rz(\theta) \to Rz(-\theta), P(\lambda) \to P(-\lambda)$).
   - **Explicit Global Phase Inversion**: To maintain strict unitary semantics across reversals, the circuit global phase is conjugated:
     $$\phi_{\text{rev}} = -\phi_{\text{fwd}}$$
   - **Barrier Preservations**: Cross-wire synchronization barriers are preserved on their affected qubits as scheduling directives.
   - **Provenance Tracking**: Every inverted operation records provenance pointing back to its original logical operation ID.
   - **Semantic Equivalence Proof**: Proven by statevector simulation that $C_{\text{rev}} \cdot C = I$ with zero phase discrepancy.
2. **Explicit Non-Unitary Operation Rejection**:
   - Measurements, resets, and classical register operations are non-unitary and cannot be semantically inverted.
   - Rather than silently treating non-unitary operations as self-inverse, `reverse_circuit` explicitly returns `Err(RoutingError::NonUnitaryReverseTraversal)`.
   - Multi-round layout refinement is mathematically restricted to unitary circuits (or unitary subcircuits); forward-only SABRE (`refinement_rounds = 1`) remains available for circuits containing non-unitary operations.
3. **Immutability of Original Circuit**: The original logical circuit $C$ is never mutated. $C_{\text{rev}}$ is constructed as a separate transactional `Circuit` instance.
4. **Reverse Routing Pass**: Routing $C_{\text{rev}}$ begins from the final layout of the forward pass ($L_{\text{fwd\_final}}$). As reverse routing proceeds, the earliest forward operations (encountered last in reverse order) naturally bring interacting logical qubits into adjacent physical positions.

### 2.2 Permutation and Layout Invariants
A critical mathematical distinction in layout-aware compilation is between layout assignments and permutations:
- A layout $L: Q \to P$ maps logical qubit $q$ to physical qubit $p$.
- At the conclusion of reverse routing, logical qubit $q$ resides at physical qubit $p' = L_{\text{rev\_final}}(q)$.
- Because reverse routing finished with the earliest operations of the forward circuit, interacting operands are now physically adjacent.
- **Fundamental Invariant**: The final layout of reverse routing is directly used as the candidate initial layout for the next forward pass:
  $$L_{\text{initial}}^{\text{candidate}} = L_{\text{rev\_final}}$$
- **No Permutation Inversion**: Inverting $L$ into $L^{-1}$ would map physical qubit IDs back to logical qubit IDs, which would corrupt asymmetric initial configurations. QCC strictly preserves layout orientation without improper inversions.

### 2.3 Iterative Layout Refinement & Multi-Metric Acceptance
Given initial layout $L_0$ (user-provided or identity):
1. **Round 1 Forward Pass**: Route forward from $L_0$, yielding routed circuit $C^{(1)}$, metrics $M^{(1)}$, and final layout $L_{\text{fwd\_final}}^{(1)}$.
2. **Iterative Refinement (Rounds $2 \dots R$)**:
   - Reverse traverse from $L_{\text{fwd\_final}}^{(r-1)}$, yielding $L_{\text{rev\_final}}^{(r-1)}$.
   - Forward traverse from candidate initial layout $L_{\text{initial}}^{(r)} = L_{\text{rev\_final}}^{(r-1)}$, yielding candidate circuit $C^{(r)}$ and metrics $M^{(r)}$.
   - **Acceptance Criterion (`is_refinement_improvement`)**: Candidate round $r$ is accepted under a hierarchical (lexicographic) decision rule strictly dominated by the composite weighted cost:
     1. Primary: strictly smaller composite weighted cost: $\text{Cost}(M^{(r)}) < \text{Cost}(M_{\text{best}})$ (by $> 10^{-12}$). A candidate with worse composite cost is rejected immediately, even if secondary metrics (e.g. SWAPs or depth) improve.
     2. Secondary tie-breaker: strictly fewer inserted routing SWAPs: $N_{\text{SWAP}}^{(r)} < N_{\text{SWAP}}^{\text{best}}$.
     3. Tertiary tie-breaker: strictly lower critical-path circuit depth: $D^{(r)} < D^{\text{best}}$.
     4. Quaternary tie-breaker: strictly lower two-qubit gate depth: $D_{2Q}^{(r)} < D_{2Q}^{\text{best}}$.
   - If accepted, update best circuit, layout, provenance, metrics, and winning round index atomically.
   - If rejected, retain current best. Rejected rounds never mutate the winning result.
3. **Telemetry Separation**: `RefinementRoundRecord` explicitly separates forward routing metrics (`forward_swaps`, `forward_depth`, `forward_cost`) from reverse traversal metrics (`reverse_swaps`, `reverse_cost`). Winning report metrics strictly reflect the winning forward pass.

### 2.4 ADR-0001 Strict Ancilla Zero-State Policy
On partially occupied devices ($n_{\text{logical}} < n_{\text{physical}}$), unused physical qubits begin in $|0\rangle$ and must return to $|0\rangle$ at circuit termination. Bidirectional SABRE preserves ancilla state across all forward and reverse iterations:
- Layout-aware semantic verification via `LayoutEquivalenceChecker` verifies the full $2^{n_{\text{physical}}}$-dimensional statevector equivalence up to a single global phase.

## 3. Implementation Overview

1. **Layout Refinement Abstractions ([`src/routing/sabre/layout_refinement.rs`](file:///home/ronanki_jitendra/rust/QCC/src/routing/sabre/layout_refinement.rs))**:
   - `RefinementRoundRecord`: Captures per-round metrics, inserted SWAP counts, critical path depths, and initial/final layout snapshots, separating forward and reverse telemetry.
   - `RefinementSummary`: Collects round progression, winning round index, winning SWAP count, winning depth, and winning cost.
   - `is_refinement_improvement`: Hierarchical multi-objective comparator enforcing primary cost dominance and 3-tier tie-breakers (SWAP count, critical depth, 2Q depth) with strict NaN rejection.

2. **Bidirectional Router & Reverse Traversal ([`src/routing/sabre/bidirectional.rs`](file:///home/ronanki_jitendra/rust/QCC/src/routing/sabre/bidirectional.rs))**:
   - `reverse_circuit`: Constructs $C_{\text{rev}}$ with inverted dependency edges, reversed topological order, exact gate adjoint inversion, parameter negation, and conjugated global phase without mutating $C$. Rejects non-unitary operations explicitly.
   - `emit_non_2q_operation`: Accurately maps 1Q gates, measurements, resets, barriers, and classical operations with full provenance tracking.
   - `SabreRouterConfig`: Encapsulates refinement rounds, maximum iterations, lookahead weight $W$, decay increment $\delta$, and optional RNG seed.
   - `SabreRouterReport`: Detailed routing telemetry containing final layout, SWAP counts, inserted operations, comprehensive metrics, and refinement summary.
   - `SabreBidirectionalRouter`:
     - `route_single_pass`: High-efficiency single-pass router executing 1Q gates eagerly, validating front-layer 2Q gates, and evaluating candidate SWAPs using `SabreHeuristic`.
     - `route_circuit`: Multi-round coordinator executing forward/reverse layout refinement, maintaining layout invariants, and returning the optimal routed circuit.
     - `validate_report`: Validates output circuit with `TargetLegalityValidator` and verifies layout equivalence against original logical circuit.

3. **Public API & Module Exports**:
   - Re-exported Phase 52 types in `src/routing/sabre/mod.rs`, `src/routing/mod.rs`, and `src/lib.rs`.

3. `test_bidirectional_sabre_derives_improved_initial_layout`: Hand-calculated 3-qubit line fixture ($CX(q_0, q_2)$ followed by $CX(q_1, q_2)$) proving reverse traversal moves $q_2$ to center $p_1$, reducing SWAPs from 1 to 0.
4. `test_refinement_acceptance_and_summary_reporting`: Verifies multi-round refinement on a 4-qubit line target, checking winning round selection and metric consistency.
5. `test_partially_occupied_device_and_strict_ancilla_zero_state`: Verifies 2 logical qubits on a 5-qubit target, proving full $2^5$-dimensional statevector equivalence via `LayoutEquivalenceChecker`.
6. `test_permutation_inversion_convention_preservation`: Proves $L_{\text{candidate}} = L_{\text{rev\_final}}$ is preserved without invalid inverse transposition ($L \neq L^{-1}$).
7. `test_asymmetric_initial_layout_permutation_and_refinement`: Hand-calculated 4-cycle asymmetric permutation ($L \neq L^{-1}$) verifying layout propagation and full layout-aware statevector equivalence.
8. `test_is_refinement_improvement_decision_hierarchy_and_tie_breaking`: Tests primary cost dominance, rejection of higher-cost candidates with better secondary metrics (conflicting metrics), 3-tier tie-breakers (SWAP count, critical depth, 2Q depth), and NaN rejection.
9. `test_refinement_rejection_preserves_winning_round_consistency`: Proves that rejected rounds do not mutate winning outputs, layout, provenance, metrics, or winning round index.
10. `test_telemetry_separates_forward_and_reverse_metrics`: Verifies that `forward_swaps`/`forward_cost` are strictly segregated from `reverse_swaps`/`reverse_cost`.
11. `test_disconnected_topology_fails_explicitly`: Verifies fast-fail `RoutingError::DisconnectedTopology` when interactions cross disconnected components.
12. `test_insufficient_capacity_fails_explicitly`: Verifies capacity check failure when logical qubits exceed physical capacity.
13. `test_deterministic_repeatability_across_identical_runs`: Verifies 100% bit-for-bit repeatability across 5 repeated runs.
14. `test_unitary_inversion_all_computational_basis_states`: Exhaustive computational basis tests ($|k\rangle \to C \to C_{\text{rev}} \to |k\rangle$) across all $2^n$ basis states for 2-qubit (4 states), 3-qubit (8 states), and 4-qubit (16 states) circuits ($28$ total basis states checked) with non-trivial global phases, mixed self-inverse, discrete adjoint, and continuous parameterized gates, verifying amplitude discrepancy $< 10^{-12}$.

16. `test_composed_operator_columns_and_frobenius_norm`: Reconstructs all 8 columns of the composed operator $U_{\text{composed}} = U(C_{\text{rev}}) \cdot U(C)$ by simulating computational basis inputs and compares against identity matrix $I_{8\times 8}$, proving Frobenius norm difference $\|U_{\text{composed}} - I\|_F < 10^{-12}$ and maximum matrix element difference $< 10^{-12}$ under one single common global phase ($0.0$).
17. `test_symbolic_parameters_and_signed_angles_inversion`: Verifies exact mathematical negation and double-reversal identity for parameterized rotations with negative angles ($-\pi/3$) and symbolic expressions (`ParameterExpr::Symbolic("alpha")` inverting to `ParameterExpr::Neg`), validating equivalence against identity using `EquivalenceChecker::with_parameter`.
