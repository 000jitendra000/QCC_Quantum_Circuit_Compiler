# Advanced Research Track: Hardware Awareness, CFG, KAK and MPS (Historical — Retired)

> [!WARNING]
> **SUPERSEDED / RETIRED — NOT THE ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT PLAN**
> The milestone and phases detailed in this document have been **retired** and are no longer active planned work.
> QCC development is currently paused after the integrated compilation pipeline checkpoint (Phase 59).
> Future development priorities and the next roadmap will be determined separately (TBD).
> This document is preserved strictly as a historical planning and architectural design reference.

> **Milestone Code**: `Research` | **Phases Covered**: 90–105 | **Status**: Retired

## Scope Summary
Optional post-v1.0 research tracks: calibration-aware costs, user-defined gates, CFG representation with dynamic branches/loops, arbitrary two-qubit KAK synthesis, Matrix Product States (MPS) simulation, and experimental optimizers.
